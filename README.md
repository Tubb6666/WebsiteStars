## 📚 What Is WebsiteStars?

WebsiteStars is a free application for your computer that helps you **save** the websites, articles, and coding resources you find online, and then **find them again** when you need them. Think of it as a personal library for the internet.

Instead of losing track of interesting links or forgetting where you read something important, WebsiteStars keeps everything organized and searchable on your own computer. You can even ask questions about your saved content and get answers.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗂️ Save Anything You Browse
While you're reading a webpage, article, or documentation, WebsiteStars lets you save it with a single click. You can add tags to organize your saves, making it simple to group related topics together.

### 🔍 Instant Local Search
Forget scrolling through endless browser history or bookmarks. WebsiteStars indexes everything you save and gives you fast, accurate search results. Find what you need in seconds, even if you only remember a word or phrase from the original page.

### 🤖 Optional AI Question-Answering
This feature is entirely optional. If you choose to enable it, you can ask WebsiteStars questions about the content you've saved. For example, "What were the key points from that article about database design?" The AI will search through your saved sources to provide an answer, with context from your own library.

### 💾 Local-First Storage
Your data stays on your computer. No cloud accounts, no syncing to third-party servers, no privacy concerns. Your saved content is accessible even when you're offline.

### 🌐 Built for Chrome
WebsiteStars is optimized for Chrome browsers (version 120 or later), so if you use Chrome, you'll get the best experience.

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

- **Tag as you save:** Adding 2-3 tags per item makes finding things much easier later.
- **Save regularly:** Make it a habit to save anything you think you might need in the future.
- **Organize by project:** Use tags like "work-project-alpha" or "home-renovation" to keep related items together.
- **Use specific search terms:** The search function works best with specific words rather than generic ones like "article."

## 🆘 Troubleshooting & Support

### The application won't start
- Make sure your Windows is up to date.
- Check that you have the latest version of Chrome installed.
- Try restarting your computer and launching WebsiteStars again.

### The Chrome connection isn't working
- Ensure Chrome is open and running.
- Check your browser extensions—make sure WebsiteStars isn't blocked.
- Re-launch WebsiteStars and try the "Reconnect" button in Settings.

### I can't find a saved item
- Double-check your search spelling.
- Try browsing by tag instead of searching.
- Check the "All Items" view in the left sidebar.
